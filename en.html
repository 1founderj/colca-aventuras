The experience

A high-altitude salt flat, without crowds

About 60 km from Arequipa, inside the Salinas and Aguada Blanca National Reserve, the lagoon transforms with the seasons: a shining salt flat in the dry season and a water mirror with Andean flamingos in the rainy season (January–March).

The Pichu Pichu, Misti and Ubinas volcanoes watch over the landscape, and along the way vicuñas, alpacas, llamas and vizcachas appear. Your 4x4 reaches where large vans cannot.

Itinerary

From sunrise to the salt flat

  1. 6:00

    Pickup in Arequipa

    We leave from your hotel in the 4x4 heading to the highlands.

  2. Chiguata · 3,000 m

    Viewpoint and breakfast

    View of Misti and Pichu Pichu with traditional breakfast among llamas and alpacas.

  3. Cacahuasi · 4,400 m

    Vicuña viewpoint

    Plateau panoramas and a gentle 20–30 min walk.

  4. Salinas Lagoon

    Salt flat and flamingos

    Walk along the shore; depending on the season, reflections or pink flamingos.

  5. ~1:00 pm

    Return to Arequipa

    End of the tour after about 7 hours of adventure.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a risk of altitude sickness?

The lagoon is above 4,000 m. Acclimatize with one night in Arequipa, drink water and move slowly.

When can you see flamingos?

There is more water and more flamingos between January and March; in the dry season the lagoon is a shining salt flat.
